# Day in Our Data: Event Program

**Saturday, October 3, 2026, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m.**  
**Meeting room, Dole Branch Library, Oak Park Public Library, 255 Augusta St., Oak Park, IL**  
**Register (free): https://oak-park.us/ciscdata**

Day in Our Data will bring residents, commissioners, Village staff, and local
technologists together to explore public data and work on useful community
questions.

The goal is not to build a finished application in four hours. A good result
could be a map, a chart, a cleaned dataset, a small prototype, or a clearly
documented question that someone can continue working on afterward. Coding is
optional.

## Schedule

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 10:30 | Organizers set up the room, project tables, signs, power, and check-in. |
| 11:00 | Welcome. Organizers introduce each starter project in about a minute; attendees pick a table. |
| 11:15 | Work block one. |
| 12:45 | Short progress check and food. |
| 1:00 | Work block two. Teams prepare something they can share. |
| 2:30 | Short demos from each team. |
| 3:00 | Wrap up and what happens next. |

Nothing is judged on the day. After the event, CISC publishes a showcase of
what every team produced (maps, charts, datasets, prototypes, and open
questions). Participants then vote on their favorites from the showcase, and
the top teams receive gift cards and are invited to present their work at a
later CISC meeting.

## Starter Projects and Your Own Ideas

Organizers have prepared eleven [starter project briefs](starter-projects/README.md),
each with a clear question, data already cached in this repo, a quick first win,
and roles that need no programming at all.

Participants may also bring their own project if it uses public data and can be
scoped to produce something shareable during the event. See the
[pitch-your-own template](starter-projects/00-pitch-your-own.md).

## What We Need Before the Event

- A participant signup page and promotional write-up.
- Starter projects with a clear question, useful data links, and a realistic
  outcome for the day.
- A shared repository or website for project information and results.
- Confirmation of room access, capacity, tables, outlets, Wi-Fi, display
  equipment, and food rules.
- A few people assigned to check-in, lead the event, help teams, and collect the
  results.
- A decision about food, supplies, LLM credits, hosting, and gift cards.

## Basic Ground Rules

- Use public data and record where it came from.
- Do not upload confidential or sensitive personal information.
- Check AI-generated code, analysis, and writing before relying on it.
- Clearly label prototypes and unfinished work.
- Nothing produced at the event is an official Village product or position.

## After the Event

We will collect each team's work and data sources and publish a showcase.
Participants will vote on their favorites from the showcase, and the top teams
will receive gift cards and an invitation to present at a later CISC meeting.
We will share useful results with the appropriate Village staff or commissions
and make it possible for interested participants to continue their projects.

## Decisions for CISC

- Approve the proposed format and schedule.
- Confirm who will handle signup, promotion, room logistics, and food.
- Confirm where project information and results will be published.
- Recruit starter projects, subject-matter contacts, and event volunteers.
