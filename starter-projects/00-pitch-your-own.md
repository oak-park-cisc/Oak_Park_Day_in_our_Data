# Pitch your own project

Got an idea the briefs don't cover? Fill this in — it takes 10 minutes — grab a table, and recruit. A project that can state its question, name its data, and pick a first step is ahead of most hackathon projects that ever existed.

## The question

> We want to know: ____________________________________________

Good questions are specific and local. Compare:
- ❌ "Analyze parking" → ✅ "Which blocks get the most parking tickets, and at what hours?"
- ❌ "Housing affordability" → ✅ "How has the price gap between a condo and a house in Oak Park changed since 2010?"
- ❌ "Village budget" → ✅ "What does the Village spend on trees, and is it going up?"

A question a neighbor would actually ask beats an impressive one.

## Why it matters

> If we answer this, ____________________________________________

One sentence. Who would change their mind, decision, or vote because of the answer? If nobody would, sharpen the question.

## The data

> We'll use: ____________________________________________

Suggestions:
- Start with the repo's `data/` folder and the [data catalog](../data/README.md) — assessments, levies, crashes, crime, Census, schools are already cached
- The Cook County open data portal (datacatalog.cookcountyil.gov) covers property, courts, health, elections and more
- No dataset? A PDF, a webpage table, or 20 minutes of manual counting IS data — some of the best civic projects start with "we typed it in"
- **Check reachability now, not at 1pm:** if the data needs a login, a FOIA request, or a scraper you haven't written, save the idea for after the event and pick something in hand today

## Quick hits — first 15 minutes

> Our first win will be: ____________________________________________

Pick something embarrassingly small that proves the data is real:
- One number ("there were N of these last year")
- One sort ("the top 10 blocks/streets/years by X")
- One chart, however ugly

If the first win takes more than 30 minutes, shrink the question.

## The build — what we'll show at 2:15

> Our demo will be: ____________________________________________

Honest options, all of which count:
- A chart or table with a finding
- A map (Google MyMaps needs zero code)
- A one-page site (ask Claude to build it around your data)
- A slide with three sentences the room will remember

Decide at the 12:50 midpoint call what you're cutting. Everyone cuts.

## Who we need

> Roles: ____________________________________________

Every team wants a **demo owner** (named now, not at 2:10), someone on data, someone on story, and someone who knows the neighborhood. Nobody needs to code to matter — the briefs' best roles are judgment calls, not syntax.
