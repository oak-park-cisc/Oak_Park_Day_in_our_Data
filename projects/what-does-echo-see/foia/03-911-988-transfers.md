Dear FOIA Officer,

Under the Illinois Freedom of Information Act (5 ILCS 140), I request the following records in electronic form, preferably CSV or Excel. I'm requesting aggregate counts only. **I'm not asking for call recordings, caller information, addresses or any individual call records.**

For calls originating in the Village of Oak Park, from the month WestCom began transferring calls to 988 to the most recent complete month:

1. The number of 911 calls transferred to 988 each month.
2. The number of those calls transferred or returned to 911, or dispatched to a police, fire or mobile crisis response, each month, if WestCom tracks this.
3. Any monthly or quarterly reports WestCom has produced on 988 transfers, as required by or prepared for the Community Emergency Services and Support Act (CESSA).

If counts for Oak Park alone aren't available, counts for all WestCom member communities would still help. Please tell me which communities they cover.

To protect privacy, please replace any count from 1 to 4 with "suppressed" before release.

If any item would require creating a new record, please tell me what existing reports WestCom has, and I'll narrow the request to those.
