## Direct request (email)

**Subject:** Questions from the Day in Our Data ECHO project

Hello ECHO team,

We're the Day in Our Data team working on "What does ECHO see?", built from the public ECHO Activity dashboard. Before anything goes to the Board of Health, we'd like to check our reading of the data with you. We'd also like to ask whether you can share a few aggregate measures that would show ECHO's work better than the dashboard can. Counts only: nothing about individual residents.

**Questions about the dashboard**
1. The dashboard counts 874 services for February to December 2025, while the Board was told about 702 referrals. Can one referral produce more than one service row?
2. What caused the September 2025 jump (178 services, mostly Police and Fire referrals)? Was it a rise in calls, a batch of follow-ups, or catch-up data entry?
3. What time does the dashboard's timestamp record, and in which time zone?

**Aggregate measures, by month, if you track them**
4. Referrals received and services logged.
5. Median days from referral to first contact. The Phase 1 update mentioned a 48-hour engagement goal.
6. How many residents were referred again within 90 days.
7. How many cases were connected to a service, and how many ECHO referrals led to shelter or housing placements.
8. Whether there's a police or fire disposition code for referral to ECHO, and what it's called.
9. Whether the Village could match ECHO follow-ups against later police or fire calls to the same household, and share only the counts. For example: "of households ECHO followed up after a call, how many had another call within 90 days".

Please replace any count from 1 to 4 with "suppressed", or tell us how you'd prefer to handle small numbers.

Thank you for your work and your time,
[Name], for the Day in Our Data ECHO team
