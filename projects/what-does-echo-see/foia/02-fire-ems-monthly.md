Dear FOIA Officer,

Under the Illinois Freedom of Information Act (5 ILCS 140), I request the following records from the Oak Park Fire Department in electronic form, preferably CSV or Excel. I'm requesting aggregate counts only. **I'm not asking for addresses, patient information, run report narratives or any individual incident records.**

**1. Monthly Fire Department and EMS responses by incident type, January 2022 to the most recent complete month.**
For each month and incident type (NFIRS incident type, EMS nature or complaint code, or whatever classification the Department uses), the number of responses. I'm most interested in:
- lift assists, invalid assists and falls
- psychiatric, behavioral and mental health calls
- overdose and poisoning calls
- welfare checks and public assists

**2. Transports.** For the same months and incident types, how many EMS responses resulted in a patient transport and how many did not.

**3. Referrals to E.C.H.O.** If the Department records referrals to the Village's E.C.H.O. program, monthly counts of those referrals by incident type.

To protect privacy, please replace any count from 1 to 4 with "suppressed" before release.

If any item would require creating a new record, please tell me what existing reports the Department has, such as monthly NFIRS or EMS summary reports, and I'll narrow the request to those. The Fire Department Organizational Assessment (Baker Tilly, September 2025) discusses non-emergency lift assists, so related summary data may already exist.

This request is for a civic data project reviewing the E.C.H.O. program for the Oak Park Board of Health. It isn't for commercial use. If fees are expected to exceed $25, please contact me first.
