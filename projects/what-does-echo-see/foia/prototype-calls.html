Call data prototype

These charts show what the team could build if the draft FOIA and data requests in this folder are answered. Every number comes from the synthetic templates in foia/templates/, which show the format requested. None of it describes real calls, real people or ECHO's real work. Dashed columns are cells the agency would hide for privacy (counts of 1 to 4): they are shown as unavailable, never estimated.
