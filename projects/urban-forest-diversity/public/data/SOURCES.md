# Data sources

## trees-oak-park.csv

- **Source URL:** https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oak-park-cisc/Oak_Park_Day_in_our_Data/main/data/trees-oak-park.csv
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-03
- **Filters:** None — full dataset as cached by the project organizers
- **Original provenance:** Oak Park Tree Inventory public view feature service (https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=792e798104b140c3b8063e86dc09d991), with `genus` derived from `latin_name` and `block` snapped to the nearest street centerline
- **Rows:** 18,837 trees
- **Columns:** `object_id`, `common_name`, `latin_name`, `genus`, `genus_source`, `dbh_in`, `height_ft`, `spread_ft`, `latitude`, `longitude`, `nearest_street`, `block`, `block_distance_ft`, `global_id`
- **Known limits:** No condition, age, or planting-year fields — DBH is the only size proxy. Blank/malformed rows must be handled client-side.

## streets-oak-park.geojson

- **Source URL:** https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oak-park-cisc/Oak_Park_Day_in_our_Data/main/data/streets-oak-park.geojson
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-03
- **Filters:** None in the file; the compute script ignores `ALLEY` segments (755) when drawing blocks
- **Original provenance:** Village of Oak Park Streets Centerlines (https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=095a733fbde4479980ee9a026728bc0b)
- **Rows:** 3,343 centerline segments with street names and address ranges

## oak-park-boundary.geojson

- **Source URL:** https://services5.arcgis.com/aymthbPDQOcCnuwg/arcgis/rest/services/Municipal_Boundary/FeatureServer/0/query?where=1%3D1&outFields=*&outSR=4326&f=geojson (Village of Oak Park Municipal Boundary, https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=6c1807a7ef5d4d77a9fbb1801d9d36d1)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-03
- **Filters:** None; one polygon (4.70 sq mi). Used only to shade the map outside the village.

## osm-parks.json

- **Source:** OpenStreetMap via the Overpass API (https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter), © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL
- **Query:** `way` and `relation` tagged `leisure=park` in bbox 41.865,-87.807,41.909,-87.774, `out geom`
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-03 (OSM data as of 2026-10-03T18:47Z)
- **Rows:** 28 park features; the 23 inside the village's block groups are kept for the map (parks across the boundary streets in Chicago and River Forest are dropped)
- **Known limits:** Community-mapped outlines, not an official Park District layer; small or unmapped green spaces may be missing

## social-vulnerability-oak-park.geojson

- **Source URL:** https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oak-park-cisc/Oak_Park_Day_in_our_Data/main/data/social-vulnerability-oak-park.geojson
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-03
- **Filters:** None
- **Original provenance:** Village of Oak Park Social Vulnerability Index, ClimateActionPlan_Service layer 35 (https://services5.arcgis.com/aymthbPDQOcCnuwg/arcgis/rest/services/ClimateActionPlan_Service/FeatureServer/35)
- **Rows:** 53 census block groups; fourteen 1–5 indices (poverty, seniors, children under 5, disability, frontline workers, no vehicle, single parent, rent burden, home cost burden, food stamps, unemployment, language, race, rented building age) and `Composite_Index`, their sum (23–56). The repo README says fifteen indices; the file has fourteen.
- **Known limits:** Indices are ranks within Oak Park, not percentages. The Village does not publish the method or vintage (underlying ACS probably 2014–2019).

## genus-family.csv (curated lookup)

- **What:** one row per genus in the inventory (68) with its botanical family, family common name and a note where classification changed or the inventory misspells a genus
- **Basis:** APG IV (Angiosperm Phylogeny Group, 2016) for flowering plants and standard conifer/ginkgo families; compiled for this project, not from a downloaded dataset
- **Changes from older references:** maples (Acer) and horsechestnuts (Aesculus) are Sapindaceae, not Aceraceae/Hippocastanaceae; lindens (Tilia) are Malvaceae, not Tiliaceae; hackberries (Celtis) are Cannabaceae, not Ulmaceae; sweetgum is Altingiaceae; tupelo is Nyssaceae; elderberry and viburnum are Adoxaceae
- **Inventory spellings mapped:** `Circidiphyllum` (Cercidiphyllum, Cercidiphyllaceae), `Syringia` (Syringa, Oleaceae)
- **Not mapped:** placeholder genera `UNKNOWN` (5 trees), `STUMP` (1) and blank (3), which get no family

## Derived: trees-oak-park-with-family.csv

- **Generated by:** `scripts/compute-blocks.mjs`; the tree CSV with `family` and `family_common` columns inserted after `genus`, joined from genus-family.csv. Same 18,837 rows and order as the source; the cached source CSV is unchanged.

## Derived: block-summary.json, tree-points.json

- **Generated by:** `scripts/compute-blocks.mjs` (`npm run compute`); regenerate after replacing any source file
- **Blocks:** the 814 hundred-blocks in the tree CSV's `block` column (e.g. "1200 N AUSTIN BLVD"), which the organizers snapped from each tree to its nearest street centerline. Names are title-cased ("1200 N Austin Blvd").
- **Block geometry:** the centerline segments (alleys excluded) whose address range covers that hundred on that street; a segment spanning two hundreds belongs to both. 810 blocks match this way; 4 streets without address ranges (Elizabeth Ct, I-290 Expy W, Home Ave, S Taylor Ave; 14 trees) use same-name segments within 80 m of their trees. `lengthFt` is the summed centerline length.
- **block-summary.json** — village stats (totals, top-10 genera and species with common names, trunk-size classes, status counts, median DBH, mean height/spread, approximate canopy, trees-per-acre correlations, priority cutoff) plus one record per block: name, tree/species/genus counts, Shannon index, top species/genus shares, top-5 genera, `status`, median DBH, mean height/spread, canopy (sq ft), street length, trees and canopy per 100 ft of street, block group, `priority`, bbox and centerline polylines; plus park outlines, block-group polygons with SVI indices, rank, fifth, land area and tree measures, and a `names` lookup for every species and genus
- **tree-points.json** — one record per tree: id, lat/lon (6 decimals), genus, common name, species id (`p`), DBH, crown spread (`w`), block index (`k`) and block-group index (`q`). The dashboard re-slices these client-side; unfiltered totals match block-summary.json.
- **Common names:** for species, the most frequent `common_name` reordered ("MAPLE-NORWAY" → "Norway Maple"); for genera, the most frequent first word of their trees' common names (Acer → Maple).
- **Canopy (approximate):** sum of π × (spread ÷ 2)² per tree; overlapping crowns are not removed, so this overstates true canopy. Public trees only (13 trees lack spread). `village.canopyCover` = 336 ac of crown over 3,003 ac of land (11.2%); `village.canopyBreaks` are the quintile cutoffs of canopy per 100 ft of street (1,486 / 2,279 / 2,993 / 3,829 sq ft) used for the map's canopy colors. The dashboard computes canopy by genus and by trunk-size class client-side for the current filters.
- **Social vulnerability:** each tree is placed in the block group it stands in (all 18,837 fall inside one). A block takes the group most of its trees stand in; streets often form block-group boundaries, so a block's two sides can differ. Block groups are ranked by composite (1 = most vulnerable, ties share a rank) and split into fifths by rank.
- **Trees × vulnerability:** compared at block-group level (53 groups; SVI does not vary within a group): trees standing in each group over the group's land area (trees per acre), and canopy over land area. `village.treesPerAcreRho` holds Spearman rank correlations with the composite and five indices (seniors −0.38, no vehicle −0.39, composite −0.31, disability −0.29, children under 5 +0.07, poverty −0.05). With n = 53, |ρ| ≳ 0.27 is p < .05; about 40 pairs were explored, so single results should be read cautiously. Diversity measures showed no notable correlation with any index.
- **Look here first (`priority`, 24 blocks):** block group in the two most vulnerable fifths, status `risk`, and canopy per 100 ft of street below the bottom-quartile cutoff across blocks (`village.lowCanopyPer100Ft`, 1,696 sq ft). Candidates for field assessment only; most are on commercial corridors (Lake St, Madison, North Ave, South Blvd, Harrison) where planting space may be limited.
- **Parks:** OpenStreetMap outlines are map context only (23 inside the village's block groups); park trees count toward their nearest street block.
- **Cleaning:** 8 rows lack DBH, 10 lack height, 11 lack spread (excluded from size averages, still counted as trees); no missing blocks or coordinates; coordinates validated against a generous Oak Park bounding box
- **Families:** 32 families. Villagewide the largest is Sapindaceae (soapberry: maples and horsechestnuts) at 22.3%, under the 30% family limit; then Fagaceae 12.8%, Fabaceae 12.2%, Ulmaceae 10.4%. 268 of 729 graded blocks have one family at 30%+ (178 of them Sapindaceae). `village.topFamilies`, each block's `topFamily` and each tree point's `f` carry family.
- **Methodology:** block status applies the 10-20-30 rule at block scale with a 10-tree minimum: `risk` = one genus ≥30% or one species ≥50% (188 blocks); `cusp` = genus ≥20%, species ≥10% or family ≥30% (517; the family test reclassifies no blocks, because every block with a family over 30% already fails the genus or species test); `meets` = within the guideline (24); `few` = under 10 trees, not graded (85). The project brief cites 164 blocks with one genus ≥30% under thresholds that were not published; with this transparent rule the count is 188, and 253 without a tree-count minimum. Village-wide checksums match the brief exactly: top species 8.8% (Acer platanoides), top genus 20.5% (Acer), 18,837 trees.
