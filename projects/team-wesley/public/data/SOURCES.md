# Data sources

All data was retrieved on **2026-10-03**. Nothing is invented. Where a source had a gap, the gap is noted below and in the detailed notes files.

| File | Source | Filter / notes |
|---|---|---|
| `homes.json` | Cook County Assessor assessed values with building characteristics, cached CSV: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oak-park-cisc/Oak_Park_Day_in_our_Data/main/data/assessed-values-oak-park.csv (from Socrata datasets `uzyt-m557` and `x54s-btds`) | Year 2025. Single-family classes 202–210, 234 and 278 only. Rows need a building size over 400 sq ft, a 2025 Board of Review total (`board_tot`) over $5,000, an address and a build year. A seeded random sample (seed 97) of 800 of the 9,831 qualifying homes. `av` = `board_tot`, which is assessed value (10% of the estimated market value), not market value. No condos (class 299), so no estimated square footage is used. |
| `homes.json` `lat`/`lon` | Cook County Parcel Universe (Socrata `nj4t-kc8j`): https://datacatalog.cookcountyil.gov/resource/nj4t-kc8j.json | Matched by PIN for year 2025. All 800 homes matched and fall inside Oak Park. |
| House photos (not stored) | Cook County Assessor property photos. The app loads them in the browser from the path the Assessor's PIN pages use: `https://prodassets.cookcountyassessoril.gov/s3fs-public/pin_detail/{PIN[0:3]}-{PIN[3:5]}/{PIN[5:8]}/{PIN}_AA.jpg` | Confirmed for one home (667×500 JPEG, dated 2020). The image server then blocked this workspace, so the other 799 could not be checked. If a photo is missing, the game links to the Assessor's PIN page instead. The Google Street View images on the Assessor's pages are not used. The Assessor's site moved from cookcountyassessor.com to cookcountyassessoril.gov. |
| `officials.json`, `bodies.json` | Official rosters from oak-park.us, op97.org, oprfhs.org, pdop.org, oppl.org and oakparktownship.org | Current elected members only; staff are excluded. Photos are linked from the official sites, not copied. Village Board photo links come from the Village Board page but could not be loaded from this workspace (403). If a photo fails to load in the browser, the game draws a cartoon icon. See `officials-sources.md`. |
| `schools.json` | op97.org school pages, oprfhs.org, IESA school listings | Colors are listed only where published (Holmes, Brooks, Julian, OPRF). Other schools use a logo tint (`logoAccent`) or a fallback color in the game. Coordinates come from OpenStreetMap Nominatim, cross-checked with the US Census geocoder. See `places-sources.md`. |
| `places.json`, `trivia.json` | Frank Lloyd Wright Trust (flwright.org), Village of Oak Park landmarks list, Park District of Oak Park, and others listed per record | 1 Tribal Council site (Village Hall), 11 challenge venues, 28 landmarks (15 by Frank Lloyd Wright). 80 trivia questions (architecture and landmarks, plus famous residents, history, transit, schools, culture and nature), each with a `sourceUrl`. See `places-sources.md`. |

Game stats, alliances and results are random and are not a judgment of any official.
